# G4 primary results

Reported as freeze (i) and (j) require: every slope beside both nulls, R^2 and
RMSE with it, a skill score the artefact cannot enter, and the panel stratified
into clean and opposed arms before any of it was read.

> **Preliminary.** Qwen thinking generation 2 stood at 963 of 976 settled and
> Codex Step 2 at 82 of 160 when these were computed. A watcher recomputes on
> completion. Nothing here is final; the Codex rows in particular rest on 12–32
> cases per arm.

## The slope, against its nulls

Seed slot 0. Cluster bootstrap over graph groups.
`results_summary/g4/primary_slope.csv`.

| model | scope | n | slope [95%] | R^2 | N1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen thinking | **clean arms (primary)** | 95 | **0.828** [0.726, 0.991] | 0.752 | 0.888 |
| Qwen non-thinking | clean arms | 95 | **0.685** [0.570, 0.846] | 0.641 | 0.888 |
| Codex | clean arms | 76 | **0.833** [0.748, 0.967] | 0.818 | 0.887 |
| Qwen thinking | opposed arms (extension) | 64 | **−0.060** [−0.134, −0.009] | 0.024 | −0.152 |
| Qwen non-thinking | opposed arms | 64 | **−0.013** [−0.089, 0.074] | 0.002 | −0.152 |
| Codex | opposed arms | 25 | **0.375** [0.083, 0.837] | 0.121 | −0.076 |

**The pooled clean-arm slope does not by itself separate the models from N1.**
0.828 with an interval of [0.726, 0.991] contains the prior-fallback reference
of 0.888. Anyone reading that slope against zero would over-claim, which is what
amendment (i) exists to prevent.

Per arm the picture is different, because both the pooled slope and the pooled
N1 are dominated by the same between-arm structure:

| model | arm | slope [95%] | N1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen thinking | `time_agnostic_t` | **1.058** [0.868, 1.244] | 0.210 |
| Qwen non-thinking | `time_agnostic_t` | 0.539 [0.327, 0.753] | 0.210 |
| Codex | `time_agnostic_t` | 0.852 [0.711, 1.005] | 0.210 |
| Qwen thinking | `event_sample_then_full_history` | 0.452 [0.211, 0.767] | 1.588 |
| Codex | `event_sample_then_full_history` | 0.662 [0.413, 0.983] | 1.588 |
| any | `node_panel_full_history` | not reportable, see (b) | — |

On `time_agnostic_t` Qwen thinking sizes the correction essentially one-for-one,
1.058, against a null of 0.210. On `event_sample_then_full_history` every model
sits far *below* its null of 1.588 — under-correcting, but nowhere near prior
fallback. Both arms separate cleanly; the pooled figure is the one that does not.

**The opposed arms fail, as (j) allowed for.** Qwen thinking is slightly negative
with an interval excluding zero — it moves the wrong way — and non-thinking is at
zero. Under (j) this is reported as a failure of quantitative weighing, not of
mechanism reasoning, because the net sign there cannot be derived from the
mechanism text without the magnitudes. That reading was fixed before these
numbers existed.

## Skill score: the discriminator

`1 - MSE(model) / MSE(best constant)`. N1 scores exactly 0 by construction.
`results_summary/g4/skill_scores.csv`.

| model | arm | hidden | direction_only | mechanism | mechanism_direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen thinking | `time_agnostic_t` | **−0.741** | −0.655 | **+0.859** | +0.783 |
| Qwen non-thinking | `time_agnostic_t` | −1.149 | −0.324 | **+0.627** | +0.683 |
| Codex | `time_agnostic_t` | −1.208 | −1.733 | **+0.831** | +0.674 |
| Qwen thinking | `event_sample_then_full_history` | +0.572 | +0.760 | +0.726 | +0.740 |
| Qwen thinking | `node_panel_full_history` | +0.871 | +0.953 | +0.943 | +0.954 |
| Qwen thinking | `recent_history_k20` | −0.700 | −0.479 | −0.666 | −0.543 |
| Qwen thinking | `time_respecting` | −1.061 | −1.270 | −0.822 | −1.136 |

Three readings, and the first is the strongest result in the study.

**On `time_agnostic_t` the mechanism description moves the skill score by about
1.6, from clearly worse than the best constant to clearly better.** −0.74 to
+0.86 for Qwen thinking, −1.21 to +0.83 for Codex. This is on the arm where a
prompt-only mechanism-aware estimator exists and reaches the truth, so the target
is achievable and the model reaches a large part of it.

**Naming the direction alone does not do it.** `direction_only` on that arm is
−0.66, −0.32, −1.73 — no better than `hidden`, and for Codex worse. The gain
comes from the process description, not from being told which way to go. That is
the question the thesis set out to ask, and the answer is not the obvious one.

**The placebo arm is passed.** `node_panel_full_history` scores 0.87 to 0.95 in
every condition and model: where no correction is needed, the models do not
invent one.

**The opposed arms never beat the best constant**, in any condition, for any
model. Consistent with the slope.

## Stated intervals: calibrated in rhetoric

The other half of the properness criterion in (h).
`results_summary/g4/stated_intervals.csv`.

| model | arm | median width | empirical coverage of nominal 90% | width vs log coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen thinking | `event_sample_then_full_history` | 0.082 | 0.463 | **−0.096** |
| Qwen thinking | `time_agnostic_t` | 0.060 | 0.272 | −0.026 |
| Qwen thinking | `node_panel_full_history` | 0.064 | 0.732 | −0.060 |
| Qwen thinking | `recent_history_k20` | 0.045 | **0.034** | +0.023 |
| Codex | `time_agnostic_t` | 0.316 | 0.737 | −0.088 |
| Codex | `node_panel_full_history` | 0.069 | 0.885 | −0.075 |

Nominal 90% intervals achieve 0.03 to 0.89 empirical coverage. Every model is
over-confident, Qwen severely so on the walk arms. Codex states intervals three
to five times wider and gets much closer to nominal. No interval is inverted.

**The two operationalizations of properness disagree, exactly as (h) said they
might.** On the clean arms the *stated* width does widen as coverage falls —
slopes −0.03 to −0.10, the direction properness requires — while the *realized*
spread across generations is flat (`docs/G4_FIRST_RESULTS_2026-09-02.md`:
+0.0008 and −0.0042, both intervals containing zero). The models say their
uncertainty grows when they know less; their answers do not become measurably
less repeatable. The prespecified phrase for this was "calibrated in rhetoric
only", and it is what the data show.

On the opposed arms even the stated width goes the wrong way (+0.012 to +0.030):
intervals narrow as coverage falls, while empirical coverage collapses to 0.03.

## What holds together

Direction and the existence of a needed correction are recovered, strongly, on
arms where one channel acts and the correction is identifiable. Magnitude is
recovered on the pure censoring arm and under-recovered on the pure selection
arm. Nothing is recovered where two channels oppose. Uncertainty is stated in the
right direction and not realized in the model's own behaviour.

The one number that would be easy to over-read — the pooled clean-arm slope of
0.83 — is the one that cannot be distinguished from prior fallback without the
R^2 and skill columns beside it.
