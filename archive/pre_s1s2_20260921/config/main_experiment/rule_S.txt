Degree-biased random walk: the initial vertex is drawn uniformly from V_full. At each step, from the current vertex u the next vertex v is chosen among the distinct neighbors of u in the full-archive undirected graph with probability d_v / (sum of d_x over all neighbors x of u), where d_x is the number of distinct neighbors of x in that graph. Event multiplicities do not affect transition probabilities. Exactly L transitions are taken, with no burn-in, no restart, and no stopping based on recovered volume. The walk stays in its starting connected component. For each dyad traversed at least once, its complete full-archive history is retrieved. Accessible zeros for listed dyads therefore indicate true inactive windows. Each observed dyad is listed once, however often it was traversed; traversal counts and vertex degrees are not reported. L is known; the full vertex count and full dyad count are unknown.
