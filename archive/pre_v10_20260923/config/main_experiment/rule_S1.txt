Degree-biased random-walk sampling is used: the initial vertex is uniform, and each transition chooses a distinct neighbor with probability proportional to that neighbor's full-archive degree. No burn-in, restart, or recovered-volume stopping is used. Every dyad reached by the walk has its complete history retrieved. Accessible zeros indicate true inactivity; each dyad is listed once. Traversal counts, revisits, degrees, walk length, and full-graph sizes are not released.
