Degree-biased random walk: the initial vertex is drawn uniformly from V_full. At each step, from the current vertex u the next vertex v is chosen among the distinct neighbors of u in the full-archive undirected graph with probability d_v / (sum of d_x over all neighbors x of u), where d_x is the number of distinct neighbors of x in that graph. Event multiplicities do not affect transition probabilities. Exactly L transitions are taken, with no burn-in, no restart, and no stopping based on recovered volume. The walk stays in its starting connected component. For each dyad traversed at least once, its complete full-archive history is retrieved. Accessible zeros for listed dyads therefore indicate true inactive windows. Each observed dyad is listed once, however often it was traversed. The table has two further columns for each pattern row: traversals is the number of walk transitions along the dyads of that row (the traversals of all rows sum to L), and inverse_degree_weight is the sum over these transitions of 1/(d_u*d_v), where d_u and d_v are the numbers of distinct neighbors of the traversed dyad's two endpoints in the full-archive graph. L is known; the full vertex count and full dyad count are unknown.
