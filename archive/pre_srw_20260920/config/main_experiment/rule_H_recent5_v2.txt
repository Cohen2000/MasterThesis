Uniform dyad sample with recent histories: n_dyads dyads are drawn uniformly without replacement from E_full. For each drawn dyad, only its most recent min(5, m_e) event records are retrieved, where m_e is its number of event records in the full archive; earlier records of that dyad are not observed. Dyads that were not drawn are not observed, and every drawn dyad is listed. n_dyads is known; the full dyad count and the dyad sampling fraction are unknown. All five windows are accessible under this rule, but a listed dyad with five retrieved records may have earlier, unobserved records, while a listed dyad with fewer than five retrieved records has none. For this rule the table has a fourth column, at_cap_dyads: the number of listed dyads with that pattern that have exactly five retrieved records.
