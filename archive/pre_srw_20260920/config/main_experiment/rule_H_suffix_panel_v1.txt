Uniform node panel restricted to the time suffix: n_panel_suffix vertices are drawn without replacement from V_full, and only dyads whose two endpoints are both in this panel are observed. Within that panel every event record in windows 3,4,5 is retrieved in full; windows 1,2 are inaccessible for every dyad. Only dyads with at least one event in the accessible suffix inside the panel are listed. Dyads outside the panel, and dyads active only before the suffix, are not observed. n_panel_suffix is known; the full vertex count and the panel sampling fraction are unknown.
