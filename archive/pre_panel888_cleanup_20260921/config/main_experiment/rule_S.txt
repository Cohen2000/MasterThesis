Simple random walk: the initial vertex is drawn uniformly from V_full. At each step, the next vertex is chosen uniformly among the distinct neighbors of the current vertex in the full-archive undirected graph. Event multiplicities do not affect transition probabilities. Exactly L transitions are taken, with no burn-in, no restart, and no stopping based on recovered volume. The walk stays in its starting connected component. For each traversed dyad, its complete full-archive history is retrieved. Accessible zeros for listed dyads therefore indicate true inactive windows. The observer receives the complete archive histories of traversed dyads and the traversal log. L is known; the full vertex count and full dyad count are unknown.
