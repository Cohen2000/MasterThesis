Walk_A has five entries A_1, ..., A_5 computed from the traversal log and retrieved full-archive histories: A_j are sums of r_e over discovered dyads with K_e=j, so A_j = sum_{e: r_e>0, K_e=j} r_e, for j=1,...,5, where r_e is the total number of traversals of dyad e across the walk, and K_e is its number of active windows in its complete full-archive history. The entries sum to L.
