Interaction-following random walk: the initial vertex is uniform on V_full. At each step, one event record incident to the current vertex is chosen uniformly from all its records in the cleaned full archive, and the walk moves to that record's other endpoint. Exactly L steps are taken, without burn-in or restart. Every traversed dyad is listed once with its complete history. Accessible zeros for listed dyads indicate true inactive windows. The table also gives inv_events, the sum of 1/m_e over dyads in the row, where m_e is the dyad's complete event count. The crawl traversal log is unavailable.
