Uniform node sampling with bounded recent history: n_panel_history vertices are sampled uniformly without replacement from V_full. Only dyads with both endpoints in that node panel can be retrieved. A common query is made at the full archive end (normalized time 1). All event records of those dyads with normalized timestamps t >= 1-History_fraction are retrieved; earlier records are inaccessible. History_fraction is a fraction of elapsed archive time, not a number of events or a fraction of each dyad's lifetime. No event-count cap is imposed. Inaccessible windows are marked ? in patterns and NA in Events_per_window. Within completely accessible windows, all event records of panel dyads are retrieved; an observed 0 in an accessible window therefore indicates true inactivity for that listed dyad. Only dyads with at least one retrieved event are listed; panel dyads active exclusively before the cutoff are also absent. The panel size and history fraction are known; the full vertex count, full dyad count, and node sampling fraction are unknown.
