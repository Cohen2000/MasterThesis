Interaction-following random walk: the initial vertex is uniform on V_full. At each step, one event record incident to the current vertex is chosen uniformly from all its records in the cleaned full archive, and the walk moves to that record's other endpoint. Exactly L steps are taken, without burn-in or restart. Every traversed dyad is listed once with its complete history. Accessible zeros for listed dyads indicate true inactive windows. The table also gives inv_events, the sum of 1/m_e over dyads in the row; traversals, the number of transitions along those dyads; and traversals_per_event, the sum of c_e/m_e over those dyads. Here m_e is the dyad's complete event count and c_e its traversal count. The traversal column sums to L.
