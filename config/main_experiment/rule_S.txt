Simple random walk: the initial vertex is drawn uniformly from V_full. At each step, the next vertex is chosen uniformly among the distinct neighbors of the current vertex in the full-archive undirected graph. Event multiplicities do not affect transition probabilities. The observer receives complete archive histories of traversed dyads and the traversal log. Exactly L transitions are taken, with no burn-in, no restart and no stopping based on recovered volume. The walk stays in its starting connected component. Walk_A records raw traversal counts grouped by the number of active full-history windows of the traversed dyad; these counts sum to L.
